About Renuka

Calm, rigorous Biology teaching for students who want clarity and confidence.

Renuka Bansal is an A Level Biology teacher with over 25 years of experience across comprehensive and grammar state schools. Throughout her career as both a teacher and Head of Department, she has helped countless students achieve outstanding examination results and unlock their full potential in Biology.

Her tutoring focuses on secure subject knowledge, precise exam technique, and the confidence students need to perform under pressure. Lessons are structured, responsive, and aligned to the expectations and patterns of the major exam boards.
